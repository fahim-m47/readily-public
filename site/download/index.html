Readily

Paste a chapter, pick a Voice, and your Mac reads it aloud.

The Voice Model behind it runs on the machine itself. Nothing you paste leaves it.

Download Readily 0.1.2

87 MB disk image, for Apple Silicon Macs on macOS 14 Sonoma or newer.

Install
Open the disk image, drag Readily to Applications, open it.
First run
Readily downloads the runtime that speaks and a first Voice Model. Together they take around 1.2 GB on disk. A few minutes, once. Narration after that is offline, and the Catalog holds the other Voices.
Updates
Readily asks at launch, and installs only when you say yes. It never replaces a newer Readily with an older one.
Privacy
Readily downloads and never uploads. Nothing you paste or narrate leaves your Mac. The update check sends no text and nothing that names you, but it runs once a launch, so the server answering it sees your IP address and the time — a record of when you opened Readily.
Your files
One folder holds the runtime, the Voice Models, the cached speech and your Narration history. Settings opens it. Deleting it resets Readily to a first run; deleting Readily leaves it behind.
Not yet
Text you paste or type, not links or files. English Voices. One Narration at a time. The Kokoro and Kitten TTS Voice Models say “read” as “red”.
