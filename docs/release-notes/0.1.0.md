Readily 0.1.0, the first release.

Paste or type text, pick a Voice, and Readily narrates it with an
open-source Voice Model running on your own Mac. Narration is offline once
a Voice Model is installed, and nothing you paste or narrate leaves it.

Not yet: Sources are pasted or typed text, not links or files. The Voices
are English. One Narration plays at a time. The Kokoro and Kitten TTS
Voice Models say the present-tense "read" as "red". Simple mode offers
only the Voices that passed curation; Advanced offers all of them.

Needs an Apple Silicon Mac on macOS 14 or newer.
